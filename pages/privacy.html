Privacy Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Version 1.0

1 Overview & Privacy First Architecture

Your privacy is central to Zoom Meeting Dock. We adhere to a local-first, minimal-data-footprint design philosophy. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not maintain tracking servers or ad-monitoring infrastructure.

2 Audio Data & Ephemeral Processing

When you record a meeting or speech note using Zoom Meeting Dock:

  • Audio-Only Capture (Zero Video): Audio is captured exclusively via navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia with audio: true and video: false. The extension never requests, accesses, or records video, webcam feeds, or screen shares.
  • Zero Continuous Audio Storage: Audio chunks are converted into WAV PCM format strictly in volatile memory and dispatched in real-time to your configured Gnani.ai / Vachana.ai STT endpoint for transcription.
  • Immediate Disposal: Once transcription completes, raw audio buffer memory is cleared and garbage collected.

3 Local Credentials & Storage

Your API Keys (e.g. Gnani STT API Key) and account credentials are saved exclusively within your browser's local sandbox (localStorage and Chrome Extension chrome.storage.local). They are never transmitted to any third-party intermediary servers.

4 Authentication & Google Sign-In

When you sign in using Google or email:

  • We only access basic profile identity attributes (such as your verified email address and display name) necessary to label personal meeting action items and deliverables.
  • We do not request or store your Google account password.

5 Transcripts & Extracted Action Items

Meeting transcripts and extracted action items reside locally in your active browser session. If you close your browser or clear site data, your local memory state is reset unless exported by you.

6 Your Control & Data Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data. At any time, you can clear your API key, sign out, reset transcript history, or uninstall the extension to permanently erase all locally held session data.
